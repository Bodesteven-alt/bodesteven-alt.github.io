The short version

Cubeloft is a single-player block puzzle game that runs entirely on your device. The game itself collects no personal data, has no user accounts, and talks to no server of ours. There is no server. Your settings, best scores, saved games, and Daily Challenge results never leave your phone.

The one exception is advertising. Cubeloft is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, and Google (not us) processes advertising data as described in section 3.

1. Data we collect

None. Cubeloft does not collect, transmit, store on any server, sell, or share any personal data. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or account credentials (there are no accounts)
  • Location data, precise or approximate, on our behalf
  • Contacts, photos, files, or any other device content
  • Usage analytics, gameplay telemetry, or crash reports sent to us
  • Device identifiers, on our behalf

We have no backend, no analytics account, and no database. There is nothing to breach, sell, or hand over, because nothing reaches us.

2. Data stored on your device only

The app stores the following on your device, in the app’s private local storage, so the game works the way you would expect:

  • Settings you choose (board size, Watch AI speed and strength, and VS AI strength)
  • Best score and best moves for each game mode
  • A saved in-progress game in the modes that support resume, so you can leave and pick it up later
  • Game statistics (games played, best results, assists used) and your Daily Challenge results history

The Daily Challenge is generated on your device from the date, so it is the same game for everyone that day with no server involved, and it works fully offline.

This data stays on your device. It is never transmitted anywhere. You can delete all of it at any time from inside the app (the Erase all data button on the Stats screen), by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app, which removes it completely.

3. Advertising (AdMob)

Cubeloft uses Google AdMob to serve a banner ad in a reserved strip on the menu and game screens, occasional interstitial ads between finished games, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for in-game assists or a revive. To serve and measure those ads, Google processes data under Google’s own policies, which we do not control:

  • Advertising ID. The Android advertising ID (or, where you have deleted or reset it, the values Google uses in its place) is used by Google to serve, cap, and measure ads, including personalized ads where you have consented or not opted out.
  • Data Google collects through the ads SDK. This can include your device model and identifiers, IP address (from which Google may infer approximate, coarse location), ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic data about ad delivery. Details are in Google’s documentation and policies:
    • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
    • Google Privacy Policy
    • Google Advertising
  • Personalization and consent. Ads are personalized according to your device settings and Google’s policies. Where consent is required before personalized ads (including the EEA and UK), it is gathered with Google’s User Messaging Platform ahead of ad loading (the consent flow is wired in the app; the GDPR message itself is configured in the developer’s AdMob account).
  • Your choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization at any time in Android Settings (Privacy, Ads). Deleting the advertising ID also limits ad measurement. These controls are provided by Android and Google and work whether or not the app is installed.

For the Google Play Data safety section, this is declared as: data handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, including the advertising ID, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, collected and shared for advertising and measurement purposes, per Google’s own SDK disclosures. No other data types are collected or shared. Data handled by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit by Google.

We do not receive your advertising ID, do not build profiles, and do not combine ad data with gameplay data (which never leaves your device in the first place).

4. Children

Cubeloft is not directed at children. The Google Play listing targets an adult audience (18+), and the app is not enrolled in the Designed for Families program. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because the app collects no data. Ad serving follows the target-audience settings declared in Google Play and Google’s requirements for the declared audience.

5. Third parties other than Google

None. The only third-party SDK in the app is the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob, section 3). The app contains no analytics services, no social features, no login providers, no cloud saves, and no other third-party SDKs that receive data. Gameplay, including the AI opponent and the Assist feature, runs entirely on your device, offline.

6. Security

Because gameplay data never leaves your device, its security is the security of your device and Android’s app sandbox. Advertising data is handled by Google under Google’s security practices; see the Google links in section 3.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what data the app handles (for example, adding a feature that needs a server), we will update this policy, change the effective date above, and describe the change here before or with the release that introduces it. The current version of the policy will always be available at this address and linked from the app and its store listing.
